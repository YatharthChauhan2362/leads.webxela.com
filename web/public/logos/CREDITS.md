# Brand logos

Real brand marks, shown only where the app works with that service (sign in buttons, sign in method on the Team page).

- google.svg: the Google "G" from Google's Sign in with Google branding guidelines, https://developers.google.com/identity/branding-guidelines
- zoho.svg: Zoho mark from Simple Icons 13.21.0 (CC0), https://simpleicons.org/?q=zoho . Zoho brand colour #E42527 is applied in the app.
- linkedin.svg: LinkedIn "in" mark in LinkedIn blue #0A66C2, drawn from LinkedIn's brand guidelines, https://brand.linkedin.com
- github.svg: GitHub mark from Simple Icons 13.21.0 (CC0)
